# SolCacao User Guide

How to use the SolCacao website as a shopper or as a business partner.

**Website:** https://solcacao-web.onrender.com

> The site runs on a free hosting plan. If nobody has used it for a while, the first page or the first sign-in can take up to a minute while the server wakes up. After that it responds normally.

## 1. Getting around

The header at the top of every page has:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shop** | Opens the gourmet catalog |
| **Wholesale** | Business services and the custom order quote wizard |
| **Build a Sampler** | Make your own box of 6 or 12 chocolates |
| **Contact** | Send a message or chat on WhatsApp |
| Magnifying glass | Search chocolates by name or origin |
| **EN / ES** | Switch the whole site between English and Spanish |
| Person icon | Sign in, or open your account once signed in |
| Bag icon | Open your cart; the number shows how many items it holds |

On a phone, the page links are inside the **Menu** button. Keyboard users can press **Tab** once on any page to reveal **Skip to main content**.

## 2. Creating an account and signing in

You can browse and fill a cart without an account. An account lets you keep your cart across devices, and a **business partner** account is needed to request wholesale quotes.

**Create an account**
1. Click the **person icon** in the header.
2. Click **New here? Create an account**.
3. Choose an **Account type**:
   - **Retail**: shopping for yourself or as a gift.
   - **Business partner**: wholesale and custom orders. You will also enter your **Company name**.
4. Choose what describes you under **I am a…** (for example *Chocolate lover*, *Pastry chef* or *Hotel / restaurant*).
5. Enter your **Full name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters.
6. Click **Create account**. A welcome message confirms you are signed in.

**Sign in**
1. Click the **person icon**.
2. Enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.

If you see *Email or password is incorrect*, check both and try again. If you see *An account with this email already exists*, use **Already have an account? Sign in** instead.

Your password is encrypted on SolCacao's server and is never stored in your browser. You stay signed in on that browser until you sign out (on the **Account** page) or for up to seven days.

## 3. Browsing the catalog

Click **Shop** to see every chocolate. The count at the top (for example *10 products*) updates as you filter.

- **Search:** type a name or a country (for example *Ecuador*) in the search box, or use the magnifying glass in the header from any page.
- **Category:** tick *Bars*, *Truffles*, *Bonbons* or *Seasonal*.
- **Cacao %:** drag the slider to set a range, for example 70% and darker.
- **Price:** drag the slider to set a budget.
- **Origin:** tick one or more countries.
- **Sort:** *Featured*, *Price: low to high*, *Price: high to low* or *Cacao: highest first*.
- **Clear all** removes every filter. On a phone, open **Filters**, choose, then tap **Show results**.

Cards marked **Seasonal** are limited releases. Click any card to open its product page.

## 4. Product pages and the cart

A product page shows the origin, cacao percentage, weight, tasting notes, price and how many are **in stock**.

1. Click **Add to cart**. The cart opens from the side of the screen.
2. In the cart, use **−** and **+** to change quantities, or the **×** button to remove an item. You cannot add more than the number in stock.
3. The **Subtotal** updates as you go.

**Guests and signed-in shoppers**
- As a guest, your cart is kept in this browser only.
- When you sign in, anything already in your cart is **added to your account's saved cart**, and later changes are saved automatically. Sign in on another device and your cart is there.
- Prices are always confirmed by SolCacao's server, so the subtotal shown is the real price.
- Signing out removes your account's cart from that browser (it stays saved in your account).

**Checkout** is a demonstration in this version: it empties the cart and shows a confirmation, but no payment is taken and no order is shipped.

## 5. Building a sampler box

1. Click **Build a Sampler**.
2. Under **Box size**, choose **6 pieces** or **12 pieces**. The price is shown for each size.
3. Under **Choose flavors**, click chocolates to fill the slots. You can pick the same chocolate more than once. The counter shows, for example, *4 of 6 filled*.
4. To change your mind, click a filled slot to empty it, or click **Empty box** to start again.
5. Optionally write a **Gift message**.
6. When the box is full, click **Add sampler to cart**. It appears in the cart as *Custom sampler* with your chosen flavors listed.

## 6. Requesting a wholesale quote (business partners)

Hotels, event planners, retailers and companies can order custom-branded chocolate. Orders start at **500 units** and typically take **4–6 weeks**.

You need to be **signed in with a business partner account** to submit. You can fill in the wizard first; if you are not signed in when you submit, the sign-in window opens, and you can submit right after signing in.

Go to **Wholesale** and click **Start a custom order**, or scroll to **Request a manufacturing quote**. The wizard has four steps; use **Continue** and **Back** to move between them.

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| **1. Cacao profile** | Pick *Bold dark*, *Floral dark*, *Fruity dark* or *Creamy milk*. |
| **2. Brand files** | Optional: **Choose file** to attach your logo or tech pack (PDF, AI, SVG or EPS, up to 20 MB). You can also send files later by email. |
| **3. Packaging** | Pick *Gift box*, *Bar sleeve*, *Pillow pack* or *Keepsake tin*, set the **Quantity** (500 to 10,000 units) and tick any **Finishes** (*Gold foil*, *Embossing*, *Ribbon*, *Recycled board*). |
| **4. Review & submit** | Check the summary and the **Estimated range**. **Company**, **Contact name** and **Work email** are filled in from your account; add a **Needed by** date and **Notes for our team** if you like. Click **Submit quote request**. |

After submitting you will see **Quote request received** with your reference number, for example *SC-7F3K2A*. Keep it for any follow-up. The SolCacao wholesale team is notified straight away and replies by email, usually within two business days.

The **Estimated range** is a guide only; the team confirms the final price in its reply.

## 7. Your account page

Click the **person icon** when signed in (or go to **Account**). You will see:

- **Profile:** your name, email, account type, role and, for business partners, your company.
- **Access:** what your account type can do.
- **Quote requests** (business partners): every quote you have submitted, with its reference, company, cacao profile, packaging, quantity and status (*Under review* until the team replies).
- **Sign out.**

## 8. Contacting SolCacao

1. Click **Contact**.
2. Enter your **Name** and **Email**, choose a **Topic** (*Retail order*, *Wholesale inquiry*, *B2B sample request* or *Support*) and write your **Message** (at least 10 characters).
3. Click **Send message**. A thank-you message confirms it was received, and the form clears. If it could not be sent, your text stays in the form so you can try again.

You can also **Chat on WhatsApp** (Monday to Saturday) using the buttons on the Contact page or the green chat button at the bottom right of every page.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *We couldn't reach the SolCacao server…* | The server is waking up. Wait a minute and try again. |
| A page or sign-in takes a long time | Same reason: the first request after a quiet period is slow. |
| *Email or password is incorrect* | Check both; passwords are case-sensitive. |
| *An account with this email already exists* | Sign in instead of creating a new account. |
| *Too many attempts…* | Wait a few minutes before trying to sign in again. |
| *Your session has expired. Please sign in again.* | Sign in again; your cart and quotes are kept. |
| *Quote requests need a business partner account* | You are signed in with a Retail account. Create a business partner account to request quotes. |
| *Choose a PDF, AI, SVG or EPS file up to 20 MB* | Check the file type and size, or skip the file and send it later by email. |
| The language changed unexpectedly | Use **EN / ES** in the header; your choice is remembered in that browser. |

## 10. What is not live yet

These parts are demonstrations in this version:

- **Checkout and payment:** no payment is taken and no order is created.
- **Newsletter sign-up** in the footer: nothing is sent.
- **WhatsApp:** the number is a placeholder, not SolCacao's real line.
- **Email:** quote and contact notifications are recorded by the server, and are delivered by email once a mail account is connected.
